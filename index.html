About Me

Applied mathematician and deep learning researcher, six-plus years at the intersection of scientific computing and generative AI.

Core expertise spans PDE&IE-based modelling, numerical analysis, and deep generative models — including CGANs and diffusion models such as DDPM, DDIM, LDM, DBSM and DBM — applied to inverse problems, computational pathology, and healthcare imaging.

Author of 7 peer-reviewed journal papers (5 of them Q1-ranked) and 7 conference papers, with 2 further works under review. Now seeking to bring this scientific ML/DL background to applied research, R&D, or scientific computing roles in industry.
